Study Protocol

This app replicates the methodology from the Kunming Institute of Zoology study (PNAS 2026): 1 ms bursts of a 1 kHz pure tone, repeated 40 times per second (every 25 ms). The study used 1 hour daily for 7 consecutive days.

This is an experimental tool based on research findings. It is not a medical device and should not replace professional medical advice. Consult a healthcare provider before use, especially if you have epilepsy or other neurological conditions.
